Time overlaps.
Space must connect.时间相遇，
空间需要连接。

Sunny heat-risk hours can overlap with PV generation and outdoor workers’ need to cool down. The service bridges the spatial gap: it finds a reachable, permitted place and prepares shade, cooling and seats when a real break becomes possible.高温的日间时段可能同时出现光伏发电与户外工作者的降温需求。服务通过可进入、可抵达的空间弥合空间错位，在真实休息机会出现时准备遮阳、降温与座位。

03 / FROM OVERLAP TO ACCESSIBLE RELIEF03／从时间重叠到可抵达的服务

An illustrative operating sequence运行逻辑示意

An approach cue triggers a readiness check. If the space is permitted and suitable, the system prepares shade and appropriate cooling and updates its open status; entry or a request confirms actual use.靠近信号触发服务准备判断；当空间获准开放且条件适宜时，系统准备遮阳和适当降温，并更新开放状态；再由进入或使用请求确认实际使用。

The value is making energy-supported relief reachable at the time of need.价值在于：让能源支持的热恢复服务，在需要时真正可抵达。

Evaluate overlap of heat-risk and energy hours, measured energy supporting cooling, access and readiness time, comfort feedback and grid use per service period.评估高温需求与可用能源时段的重叠、实际供给制冷的电量、可进入性与准备时间、舒适反馈及每段服务的电网用电。
